# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Possess any citizen; walk, run and punch. Some citizens fight back with the game's own punch and kick animations.
- Steal parked or moving cars and drive them; traffic follows the city's real roads.
- Run people over (they fly, hits count up a combo); wanted stars, police on foot, WASTED and BUSTED.
- People and cars are drawn by the game's own sprite system, so buildings, trees and highways hide them. An
  outline shows you and your car when something covers you. Buildings and trees block walking and driving.
- Works in every city: map, roads and buildings are read from the running game; sprites come from your install.
- Switches itself off on game builds other than the Steam release's April 2000 DLLs.
- Developer command channel (memory tools, input, filming commands) only when `Apps\gta_mode.dev` exists.
